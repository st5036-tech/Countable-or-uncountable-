Game Setup & Flow

  1. Divide the classroom into 5 distinct teams.
  2. Click on a Team card on the top bar to mark whose turn it currently is.
  3. Let the active team choose any numbered supermarket card on the grid.
  4. Give teams approximately 20–30 seconds to discuss answers in English.
  5. Click "Show Answer" to verify and award points accordingly.
  6. Apply any special Power-Up actions (like Point Thief or Double Trouble) using the prompt buttons.
  7. Once all 20 cards are opened, click the "Final Boss" button for the conclusion challenge worth 25 XP per team!
